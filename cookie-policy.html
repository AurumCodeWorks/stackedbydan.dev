Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 April 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit, making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

2. How We Use Cookies

stackedbydan.dev uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They include:

  • cookie-consent — Stores your cookie consent preference. Expires after 1 year.

2.2 Analytics Cookies (with consent)

Where you have given consent, we may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with the website. This helps us improve the user experience. These cookies may be provided by third-party services such as Google Analytics.

  • Data collected: pages visited, time on site, referral source, browser/device type
  • Data is anonymised where possible
  • No personally identifiable information is collected via analytics cookies

2.3 Third-Party Cookies

Google Fonts is loaded from Google's CDN. Your browser may set cookies associated with Google's services when this resource is loaded. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit the site, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can also manage your consent preferences directly from this page at any time:

Status: checking...

Alternatively, you can clear your browser cookies, which will reset the preference and show the banner on your next visit.

4. Browser Cookie Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. To find out how to manage cookies in your browser, visit the browser's help pages:

  • Google Chrome — Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Firefox — Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings > Cookies and site permissions
  • Safari — Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data

Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.
