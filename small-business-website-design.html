What you actually get

> Every build is written by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no WordPress underneath, no theme, no page builder and no stack of plugins waiting to break. That is why these sites load almost instantly, and why they keep working when nobody is maintaining them.

> Included as standard on every package, and not counted against your page limit: a privacy policy, a cookie policy, terms and conditions, a cookie consent banner, an XML sitemap, a robots.txt file and submission to Google Search Console. These are the things cheap builds quietly leave out, and the ones that cause problems later.

> You also get the domain and DNS set up properly, HTTPS, and hosting configured on Cloudflare's global network so the site is fast wherever your customers are.
