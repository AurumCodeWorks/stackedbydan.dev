What gets checked

Speed & Core Web Vitals

Real load timings, Largest Contentful Paint, layout shift and responsiveness, measured on both mobile and desktop rather than guessed at.

Mobile experience

How the site behaves on a phone: tap target sizes, readable text, horizontal overflow, and how far a visitor must scroll before reaching the point.

Technical SEO

Titles, descriptions, heading structure, canonical tags, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data, redirects, and whether Google can index you at all.

Accessibility

Colour contrast, keyboard navigation, image alt text and form labels. This is both a legal consideration and a straightforward usability win.

Security & headers

HTTPS configuration, security headers, mixed content, exposed versions and out-of-date dependencies that are quietly putting you at risk.

Conversion basics

Whether a visitor can tell what you sell, how quickly they reach a price, and how easily they can contact you. Often worth more than any technical fix.

What usually turns out to be wrong

> Enormous images. Easily the most common. A photo straight off a phone at several megabytes, scaled down in the browser rather than resized properly. One afternoon of work often halves a site's load time.

> Too much code for the job. A page builder, a theme and a stack of plugins all loading on every page, including pages that use none of it.

> Nothing above the fold. Visitors arrive and cannot tell within a few seconds what you do, where you are, or what it costs, so they leave. No amount of speed work fixes that.

> Indexing problems. Sites that cannot rank because of a stray noindex tag, a blocked robots.txt, duplicate pages competing with each other, or a migration done without redirects.
